Financial Year
All Years

Closing a year does not move any balance. Stock, customer dues, supplier dues, cash and bank continue into the new year exactly as they are — there is nothing to carry forward. Closing records what the year finished at, so you can always see the 31 March position again, and it locks the year so the figures you filed cannot change. Bills, reports and exports for a closed year keep working as normal.
