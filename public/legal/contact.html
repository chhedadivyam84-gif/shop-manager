The Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025 set their own timelines from May 2027. Confirm both.

Making a request

Tell us which business you use Shop Manager with, what you are asking for — to see, correct or remove information — and how we can reach you. To protect your records from someone pretending to be you, we may ask you to confirm a detail that only the business would know, such as the registered phone number, before we act.

Some information has to be kept even after a request to remove it — invoices and accounts in particular, which GST law requires a business to keep. We will tell you what, and why. See the Data Retention and Deletion Policy.
