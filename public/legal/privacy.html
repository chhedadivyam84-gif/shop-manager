Privacy Policy

Last updated 21 October 2026 · Takes effect [EFFECTIVE DATE] · Run by [BUSINESS NAME]

This policy explains, in plain words, what information Shop Manager holds, why, where it is kept, how long, and what rights you have. It is written for India, under the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

1. Who this is about

Two kinds of information pass through Shop Manager:

  • About you, the shop owner, and your staff — your business details and the people who sign in. We collect these to give you the service.
  • About the people in your records — your customers, suppliers and employees. You enter this information and decide how it is used. We keep it and process it only for you, to run the software.

If you are a customer of a shop that uses Shop Manager, that shop is responsible for your information. Please ask the shop first (see section 9).

3. Why it is collected, and how it is used

  • To make bills and GST invoices, keep stock, and keep track of who owes what — the things Shop Manager is for.
  • To sign people in, and let each staff member see only what the owner allows.
  • To keep your subscription running, and to contact you about your account, payments or the service.
  • To keep your records safe: backups, and finding and stopping misuse.
  • Only if you turn them on: to answer questions with the Assistant, or read a supplier bill with Bill Scanner (see AI & voice).

We do not use your records for anything else.

4. We do not sell your data

  • We do not sell your information, or your customers’ information, to anyone, and we do not share it with anyone for their own marketing.
  • Shop Manager contains no advertising and no analytics or tracking of any kind.
  • It does not record or keep audio.

6. Telling your own customers

This is your responsibility as the shop owner. When you store a customer’s name, phone number and purchases in Shop Manager, you are the one collecting their information. Under Indian law you should:

  • tell your customers that you keep their details and bills in billing software, and why — for example with a notice at your counter, or a line on your bills;
  • use their details only for your shop’s business — billing, delivery, and keeping track of what is owed;
  • answer them if they ask to see, correct or remove their details (Shop Manager lets the owner do this — see section 9).

7. How long it is kept, and how it is deleted

  • While you subscribe, your records are kept until you delete them.
  • Customers and suppliers with no bills can be deleted by the owner. One who appears on a past bill cannot be deleted, because the law requires invoices to be kept complete. They can be corrected, or marked inactive.
  • Backups are kept for up to 12 months, then deleted automatically.
  • The sign-in cookie lasts up to 30 days, or until you sign out.
  • When you leave, you can export your records for [EXPORT DAYS] days. We then delete them from the live service within [DELETION DAYS] days; copies in backups go as those backups expire.

The full details are in the Data Retention and Deletion Policy.

8. How it is protected

These are steps the software really takes. No system is perfectly secure, so they are not a guarantee.

  • Each business’s records are kept in a separate database, and one shop can never open another’s.
  • Staff see only the parts the owner has allowed; this is checked on the server, not just hidden on screen.
  • PINs and passwords are stored only as one-way hashes. API keys are stored encrypted.
  • The hosted service works only over HTTPS. Repeated wrong PINs are slowed down and locked out.
  • Error logs record what went wrong and where — not the details that were being typed.
  • Changes are written to an activity record that cannot be edited afterwards.

There is one cookie, for staying signed in. It holds a random identifier, not personal details; it cannot be read by scripts on the page; and it lasts up to 30 days. It is needed for the software to work, so there is no setting to refuse it.

9. Your rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 you have the right to:

  • Access — ask what information is held about you, and how it is used;
  • Correction — have wrong or incomplete information fixed;
  • Deletion — ask for information to be removed when it is no longer needed;
  • Withdraw consent — where information is used because you agreed, withdraw that agreement at any time (this does not undo what was done before);
  • Complain — to our Grievance Officer (section 10), and then to the Data Protection Board of India.

If you are a customer of a shop, ask that shop. Its owner can show, correct, or delete your details, or mark them inactive where past bills must be kept. If you are a shop owner or staff member, contact our Grievance Officer.

Some information must be kept even after a request to delete it — invoices and accounts in particular, which GST law requires a business to keep. We will tell you what has to stay, and why.

Most of these rights come fully into force on 13 May 2027, when the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025 take full effect. You can already make these requests to us now.
