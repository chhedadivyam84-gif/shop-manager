Data Retention and Deletion

Effective [EFFECTIVE DATE]

How long Shop Manager keeps each kind of information, why, and what deleting something actually does. Some records cannot simply be deleted, because the law requires a shop to keep them; this page says which, rather than promising more than the software does.

The rule that overrides the rest

GST law requires a registered business to keep its accounts, invoices, credit and debit notes and delivery challans for 72 months from the due date of the annual return for the year they belong to — and longer while an appeal, proceeding or investigation about them is open. Shop Manager is built so that those records are not lost by accident: a customer or supplier who appears on a past bill cannot be deleted, and a product that has been sold can only be deleted by the owner on purpose — and even then the past bills keep the product’s description on each line.

Section 36 of the CGST Act, 2017 and Rule 56 of the CGST Rules, 2017; and whether income-tax or other record-keeping rules require any record to be kept longer.

Deleting

A customer or supplier

The shop’s owner can delete a customer or supplier who has no transactions. One who appears on any bill, payment or other record cannot be deleted — the software refuses, so that past invoices stay complete. They can instead be corrected, or marked inactive so they no longer appear in day-to-day use.

Deleting removes the record itself. The fact that it was deleted — and the name it had — stays in the activity record, which cannot be edited.

The activity record keeps names permanently, including after the record they belonged to is deleted. This supports accountability and fraud prevention, but it may need to be reconciled with a person’s right to have their data erased under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

A business’s account

A business that stops using Shop Manager can ask for its data to be exported and then removed, using the details on the Contact page.
